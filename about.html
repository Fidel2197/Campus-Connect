About

A student connection platform for tutoring, events, groups, skill exchange, and campus networking.

Campus Connect is a student-built platform designed to make campus life feel smaller, friendlier, and easier to navigate. It brings tutoring, events, groups, messaging, and skill-sharing into one place so students can find what they need without digging through endless group chats or flyers.

At the core of Campus Connect are student profiles. Each profile highlights a student’s major, year, skills, interests, availability, and what they’re looking for, whether that is a tutor, a study partner, new friends, or networking opportunities. By organizing this information in a simple, structured way, the platform makes it easier to connect with the right people at the right time.

Academic support is a big focus. Campus Connect includes a tutoring and request hub where students can post offers or create requests by course, level, preferred times, and budget. Students can browse, filter, and match with options that fit their schedule and needs.

The platform also supports recurring groups like study circles, project teams, and clubs. Built-in messaging helps students contact tutors, event hosts, and group members directly, while the campus map ties activity back to study spots and meeting spaces.

Why We Built It

Campus Connect started from a simple question: why is it so hard to find the right people and resources when they’re already on the same campus? We saw classmates struggling to locate tutors, events with open seats that students never heard about, and groups that wanted more members but didn’t have a central place to reach them.

This project was created to address that gap by combining profiles, tutoring, events, groups, messaging, and maps into a single experience built from a student perspective.

The experience is designed to help students succeed academically, meet people they might not otherwise cross paths with, and feel more at home on campus.
